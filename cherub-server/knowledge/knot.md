# Knot product knowledge

Knot is a private, mutual-first campus social app for eligible users aged 18–21.

Core areas include Welcome and profile setup, Discover, Matches, text-only Trial Chat, Activity/Notifications, Profile, Incognito, Secret Crush and Couple's Mode for committed users.

Discover is same-city. Users can pass, express interest, or use Secret Crush after viewing the relevant card details. Secret Crush is private unless a legitimate mutual-match condition is met.

Knot uses age/identity verification and blocks users who are not eligible. Profile setup includes city and university selection, with Other options where needed.

Cherub must not reveal another user's private actions, private chats, Secret Crushes, passwords or hidden data. Cherub can explain features and suggest wording, but does not send messages on the user's behalf unless an explicitly authorized app tool is later provided.
